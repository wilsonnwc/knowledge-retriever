Four questions executives use to evaluate your roadmap:

**"What is it?"**
"This is [name]. It helps [who] do [job] by [mechanism], so [observable business outcome]."

**"Why does it matter?"**
Focus on business outcome: grow revenue, reduce cost, protect margin, reduce risk. NOT product metrics.
"This matters because it moves [business lever] via [mechanism], measurable by [metric] in [time]."

**"When do I get it?"**
Not "when to get this feature", but:
- When does it start to matter?
- When do we learn if it works?
- When can we stop or pivot?
- When do we get value?

"We get the first signal by [date], confidence by [date], and a scale-or-stop decision by [date]."

**"How much does it cost me?"**
What we are spending, what are we NOT doing.
"This costs [build effort] plus [ongoing run and maintenance], with the tradeoff that we delay [other work]."

> **Why this matters:** Execs speak the language of business levers, timelines, and trade-offs. Frame your roadmap in their terms, not yours.
