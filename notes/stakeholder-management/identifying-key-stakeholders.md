Not everyone trying to have a say in the product is a key stakeholder—they could be just part of the community.

Those who have veto power, or can prevent a product from launching, are the true stakeholders:
- Executive team
- Business partners
- Finance
- Legal
- Compliance
- Business development (ensuring the new product won't violate existing contracts)

**Action:** Always try to speak to stakeholders before a product is built. Speak with evidence, not opinions.

> **Why this matters:** Veto power defines stakeholders. Engage them early with evidence, not persuasion.
