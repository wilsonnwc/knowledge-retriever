title: Do not present to a large group with just a PowerPoint
author: 
---
It's not a good idea to have one large group meeting with all stakeholders and present with a PowerPoint.

Why:
- Different stakeholders need to see different things in detail, particularly how the product works
- A group setting tends to produce design-by-committee results, which is usually mediocre

Better approach:
- High-fidelity user prototypes are ideal
- Prototypes > PowerPoint
- One-on-one or small group conversations allow stakeholders to ask targeted questions about what matters to them

> **Why this matters:** Committee meetings flatten nuance; prototypes let each stakeholder see what they need to care about.
