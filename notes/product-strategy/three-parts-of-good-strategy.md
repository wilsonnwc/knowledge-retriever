Good strategy has three parts:

1. **Diagnosis** — a clear-eyed assessment of the challenge. What's actually going on? What's the critical obstacle?

2. **Guiding policy** — an overall approach for dealing with that challenge. Not a goal, but a governing constraint or direction.

3. **Coherent actions** — specific, coordinated steps that follow from the policy. Everything should reinforce everything else.

> **Why this matters:** A framework to distinguish real strategy (with diagnosis, policy, and coherent action) from mere goal-setting.
