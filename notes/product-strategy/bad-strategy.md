What makes strategy "bad":

* **Fluff** — vague, buzzword-laden language that sounds strategic but says nothing
* **Failure to face the challenge** — avoiding the hard diagnosis and just stating objectives instead
* **Mistaking goals for strategy** — "we will grow revenue 20%" is not a strategy
* **Bad strategic objectives** — goals that are either impossible, irrelevant, or don't address the real problem

> **Why this matters:** Know the common failure modes: fluff, denial, goal-chasing, and misaligned objectives. Clarity means naming the real constraint.
