Recognize that creating clarity for yourself and your team will require navigating a lot of uncomfortable conversations. Learn to treat discomfort as a valuable signal of potential misalignment, not as something to be avoided or minimized.

> **Why this matters:** Discomfort in conversations signals misalignment; lean in, don't avoid it.
