Seven stages of action:

1. What do I want to accomplish? 
2. What are the alternative action sequences? 
3. What action can I do now? 
4. How do I do it? 
5. What happened? 
6. What does it mean? 
7. Is this okay? Have I accomplished my goal?

> **Why this matters:** A mental model for how users think through tasks; reveals where design can help bridge the gulf between intention and execution.
