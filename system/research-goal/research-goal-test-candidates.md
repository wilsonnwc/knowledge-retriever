# Research-Goal Test Candidates

A running list of test goals for manual testing of `research_goal()`. Add new candidates anytime you think of a realistic goal to test.

---

## Current Test Candidates

### Goal 1: Stakeholder Communication
**Goal:** Improve communication with all stakeholders — including senior leadership, my team, and other cross-functional teams.

**Why this goal:** Tests a broad, foundational PM skill with notes spanning communication, leadership, and stakeholder-management folders. Good for testing multi-round research on a general PM competency.

**Expected note categories involved:** communication, leadership, stakeholder-management, product-organisation

---

### Goal 2: Design Judgment
**Goal:** Make good design judgment.

**Why this goal:** Tests design-specific knowledge with narrower scope than Goal 1. Good for testing whether the feature can narrow to a specific domain (design folder + adjacent content).

**Expected note categories involved:** design, product-strategy (may touch on design decisions)

---

### Goal 3: AI PM Skills & Frameworks
**Goal:** What are the core AI PM skills and decision-making frameworks I need for an AI product manager role?

**Why this goal:** Tests cross-domain synthesis across ai-products, ai-general, product-strategy, and design. More specific than Goals 1–2, but still requires pulling together concepts from multiple areas. Good for testing whether research-goal can handle "AI-specific angle on a general PM topic."

**Expected note categories involved:** ai-products, ai-general, product-strategy, design, leadership

---

## Notes on Adding New Candidates

- **Breadth vs. Specificity:** Mix broad goals (like #1) with narrower ones (#2) and cross-domain ones (#3)
- **Realistic scope:** Goals should be something you'd actually want to research using your notes
- **Coverage:** Try to test different subsets of your note collection over time
- **Format:** Copy the template above (Goal Name, Goal text, Why this goal, Expected categories)
